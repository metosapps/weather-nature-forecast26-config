Privacy Policy

Updated 28 October 2026

This policy covers Weather Nature, Android package app.weather.metaapps.forecast26. Contact for this app: contact@agafaycamels.com.

Weather and location

You can search for and save a city manually. If you allow location access, the app can use your device location to find local weather. City identifiers, search text or coordinates are sent to the weather provider, AccuWeather, to obtain forecasts. Network requests also reveal your IP address to the service receiving them. The app may use ip-api.com to estimate a location from your IP address.

Saved cities, display units, notification preferences and cached weather are stored on your device. Widgets and enabled weather notifications may refresh weather for your saved location while the app is not open. You can manage location and notification permissions in Android Settings.

App services and advertising

The app uses Firebase services for weather configuration, app analytics and push messaging. These services may process installation identifiers, device and app information, usage events and network information. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

When advertising is enabled, Google advertising services and enabled advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, ad interactions and consent choices to deliver, measure and protect advertising. The app includes mediation integrations for advertising partners such as AppLovin, Meta and Mintegral; which services are used depends on the app build and active configuration. Advertising is currently disabled in this app. If advertising is enabled in a test build, only test advertisements are used. Device privacy controls and any available consent controls apply independently of remote settings.

Optional purchases and Samsung billing

New purchases are currently disabled and no product catalog is connected. If offers become available, the app may offer paid ad removal through Samsung Galaxy Store. Samsung handles checkout, the Samsung account and the payment method. The app receives information needed to recognize a purchase, such as the product ID, purchase or order identifier, transaction result and any subscription availability or expiry information. The app does not receive your full payment-card number or Samsung account password. See Samsung privacy information.

The app uses Samsung IAP services to check ownership, restore eligible purchases and acknowledge delivery of paid benefits. Purchase status and related identifiers may be stored on your device to maintain access and retry unfinished processing. Connection information is also visible to Samsung when these services are used. Purchase identifiers are not advertising identifiers and should not be posted publicly. If you contact support about a purchase, share only the receipt information needed to investigate and hide unnecessary personal or payment details.

Remote settings and public pages

The app downloads a small public configuration file hosted on GitHub to obtain app links, update notices, maintenance information and supported feature settings. That request exposes ordinary connection information, including your IP address, to the hosting provider. It does not send your saved cities, precise location or personal purchase records in the configuration request. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Configuration cannot install an APK or download executable app updates; updates open the appropriate app-store listing.

Support and information you choose to share

Opening Contact support launches your email app. An email is sent only when you choose to send it. We receive the address, message and attachments you send and use them to respond and investigate your request. Do not include passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. Sharing the app launches Android sharing controls.

Retention and your choices

You can remove saved locations, turn off optional notifications, deny location permission or clear app data in Android Settings. Clearing local app data does not automatically delete records held by external service providers or cancel a Galaxy Store subscription. Manage any subscription in Galaxy Store; eligible purchases can be restored using the same Samsung account. Service providers' retention and processing are governed by their policies; AccuWeather information is available in its Privacy Policy. To ask about data associated with this app or request access, correction or deletion where applicable, contact contact@agafaycamels.com. We may need information to identify your request and verify it.

Security, children and changes

We use available platform safeguards and restrict remote app settings to data. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us. This policy may change as the app changes; the current online policy is linked from Help & legal. The app also includes an offline copy that reflects its build date.
