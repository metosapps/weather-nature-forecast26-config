Quick answers

Can I choose a city without sharing my location?

Yes. Open the side menu and manage your locations to search for and save a city. Device location permission is optional; you can use a saved city without it. You can change permissions in Android Settings.

Why is the weather not updating?

Check your connection and selected city, then try refreshing. Saved weather can be older than current conditions. If the weather provider is temporarily unavailable, try again later. Our Help page has more guidance.

Where can I change temperature and other units?

Open the side menu for temperature and unit settings. Your display preferences are saved on your device.

How do I add a weather widget?

Open Weather widgets from the side menu, select a preview and follow your launcher's prompt. You can also use your home-screen widget picker. Launcher support and Android battery restrictions can affect widgets and their refreshes.

Does the app currently show ads or sell upgrades?

Advertising and paid upgrades are currently disabled. If paid ad-removal offers become available, the app will show the actual offers and terms returned by Samsung Galaxy Store before you confirm a purchase.

How can I restore an eligible Samsung purchase?

If purchases are available in your build, use Restore purchases in the purchase screen while signed in to the same Samsung account. Restoration checks eligible ownership for this app without making a new payment. See Help for restoration and subscription guidance; contact us before buying again if a valid purchase is missing.

Where can I read about my data and choices?

The Privacy Policy explains weather and location requests, app services, local data and support information. You can manage Android permissions in device settings. Email us if you have a question or a request about data associated with this app.
